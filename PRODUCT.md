# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: a household operator (the owner / family) who opens the app day-to-day on phone or desktop to keep household operations current—subscriptions, bank/e-ticket balances, food inventory, documents, routines, and media queues.

Job on a typical visit: check what needs attention (expiring food/subscriptions, balances, notifications) and act quickly—update records, open a module, queue media, or clear a reminder.

## Product Purpose

鋒兄AI管理系統 is a bilingual (zh-TW primary) household digital console: one shell for subscriptions, bank balances, food inventory, notes/documents, shared accounts, media (images, videos, music, podcasts), voice commands, finance/news tools, and FFmpeg-backed media utilities (image + voice → video).

Success looks like: the operator can trust status at a glance, complete common CRUD and media tasks without fighting the UI, and return often because the console feels calm and fast rather than noisy.

## Positioning

A calm household command center—everything that keeps the household running is clear, trustworthy, and quick to update.

## Brand Personality

Calm · Editorial · Gold

Tone: restrained, bilingual-capable UI copy; editorial hierarchy over “AI dashboard” chrome. Warm gold-amber brand energy without fintech cliché or starter-kit purple.

## Anti-references

- AI starter kit: purple/blue gradients, generic SaaS metric cards, glassmorphism as decoration, “Boost your productivity” copy
- Identical icon+title+body card grids and template hero-metric layouts
- Gradient text, side-stripe accent borders, over-rounded (32px+) cards
- Visual schizophrenia: token glass shell mixed with gray/blue hardcode modules

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.2 AA for text contrast, focus-visible controls, and meaningful control labels
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for decorative motion (confetti, hover translates); do not break media player controls
- Touch: practical 44px minimum on coarse pointers for primary actions; compact density may tighten tables but not primary CTAs

## Design Principles

1. **Status before chrome** — Surface what needs attention (expiry, balance, due dates) before decorative shell flourishes.
2. **Trust through clarity** — Numbers, labels, and state language stay unambiguous; never hide critical status in gradient noise.
3. **One visit, one primary action** — Prefer short paths to update or open the relevant module over multi-step ceremony.
4. **Shared vocabulary across modules** — Same household language and interaction patterns from subscription to bank to media so the console feels one product.
5. **Polish without breaking media truth** — Visual refinement never changes playback, upload, or FFmpeg behavior.
